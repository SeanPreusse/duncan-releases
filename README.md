# Duncan

**Your memory. Your choice of AI.**

Duncan brings your documents, notes, and ideas into a local library you own. Connect compatible AI tools through MCP and bring your knowledge into the conversation.

## What Duncan does

- Keeps original documents and searchable knowledge on your Mac.
- Finds source passages using keyword and on-device meaning search.
- Shares selected context with compatible local MCP clients such as Claude Desktop, Cursor, and Codex.
- Exports and imports your library so your knowledge stays portable.

Start with non-sensitive sample documents and keep backups. Duncan does not encrypt its library. Connected AI tools and optional analysis providers may receive content you choose to share.

This repository distributes installers and release notes. Application source is maintained separately.
